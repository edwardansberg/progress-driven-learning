# Maintain the learning framework

Scope: Changes to reusable rules, templates, record formats or future workspace software. Ordinary explanations, practice, materials and closeout follow [learning policy](../README.md); learners inherit no release cycle or branch-per-session requirement.

## Development record

Use one optional docs/maintenance/progress.md for framework development only. Before writing, inspect identity, remote, branch/HEAD, staged/unstaged/untracked changes, worktrees, applicable instructions and existing maintenance state. Read affected sources and preserve unrelated work, local constraints, history and pending decisions. Resume compatible work; resolve a collision instead of overwriting it.

Create/reuse a topic branch only under applicable authority and after checking ownership/state. An initial task may authorize safe branch setup and direct implementation after a recorded plan. Do not request the same authority twice. A small reversible correction needs proportionate inspection; it need not create a full development scaffold.

Record goal/scope, human authority and exclusions, base/candidate identity, bounded plan, keep/rewrite/relocate/remove/add map with owners, criteria, risks, checks and next owner/gate in that record. Keep one current maintenance plan; supporting material is subordinate. Learner context, roadmap, units and progress are never framework-development status files.

For a migration that changes existing progress-file meanings, first record the plan in the currently authoritative record. Move its complete active content and unique summary evidence to the identified maintenance record, repair links, establish that authority, then initialize neutral learner files. Do not erase pending review to produce a clean-looking template.

## Implement and review

Proceed within recorded human scope. Pause only the affected work for a material missing decision or conflict; state its source/consequence and continue independent authorized steps. New policy text grants no new authority.

Verify the complete changed/new/deleted candidate: links/anchors, entry routes, single fact/procedure ownership, neutral starters, matching overview revision, separate maintenance state, preserved original attempts, example isolation, portable material references and dependencies of removed files. Use focused checks and labeled static walkthroughs, with basis, scope, result and limits in the same record. No new validation platform is required.

Use Planning → Implementing → Ready for user validation when direct execution is authorized; otherwise record the pending scope approval before implementation. Human acceptance remains a separate observation. Stop at the requested gate; successful checks do not grant delivery rights. Staging, commit, push, PR, merge, deployment, settings changes and automation require their applicable named authority under [publication rules](../../AGENTS.md#publication-authority); this starter includes no standing owner grant. Attribution follows [agent guidance](../../AGENTS.md#hygiene-and-authorized-commits).

## Later authorized release packaging

Packaging/publication are separate from local implementation and human acceptance.

1. Under separately granted authority, preserve the complete maintenance record and retained support in a durable reachable evidence checkpoint. Record its exact revision/path in release or review evidence; ensure it survives branch removal or the chosen integration strategy. Do not rely on an uncommitted disposable copy or delete the only evidence.
2. Prepare a later forward packaging change excluding maintainer-only active/history records from the starter tree. Preserve them at the checkpoint; repair candidate-specific links to records omitted from the release. Keep unresolved human review/delivery states in the preserved evidence.
3. Produce neutral context, roadmap, detailed state and matching overview with no imported learner facts, attempts, approvals, automatic follow-up or maintainer status. Keep clearly labeled synthetic example and prepared/unrun pilot. Keep the canonical derivation block; future learner adoption is recorded separately in context.
4. Inspect the entire proposed release tree, including unchanged files, for privacy, neutrality, links and usable start/resume routes. Preserve existing learner evidence in any real workspace; neutralization is only for release packaging.
5. Obtain human review of the identified candidate and separate authority for each publication action. Record actual release artifact and checks; do not infer publication from acceptance or a packaged tree.

Without checkpoint/publication authority, leave the real maintenance record and pending review intact in the local candidate. If corrections follow packaging, recover the preserved record, reconcile later decisions and continue there; do not open a competing plan.

GitHub template generation is conditional on a later authorized owner enabling/verifying the setting. Settings remain a separate action; the framework does not enforce repository protection or claim a template-generation test.
